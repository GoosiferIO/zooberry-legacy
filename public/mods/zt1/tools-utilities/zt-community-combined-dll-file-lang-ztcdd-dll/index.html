    ZT Community Combined dll File (LANG--ZTCDD.dll)

    This file contains messages that are meant to be used by multiple sites in the community, either for ztd files that are put at multiple sites or for messages that are reused for different ztd files at different sites. Many messages in it are used by the downloads at the Designer’s Guild (DG) site, which is a site where designers from multiple sites meet, along with at the Designer’s Directory (DD) site.

    NOTE: The file at this site is a backup. Before downloading from here, you should check the link at the end of this post to see if that is newer.

    Compatibility: All Game Versions

    Description: This file contains messages that are meant to be used by multiple sites in the community, either for ztd files that are put at multiple sites or for messages that are reused for different ztd files at different sites. Many messages in it are used by the downloads at the DG site, which is a site where designers from multiple sites meet, along with at the DD site.

    This dll contains messages for the following:

    Buildings: Africa, the Dark Continent, Exhibit Aloha Gifts Animal Nursery Antarctic Discovery Center Arabian Wildlife Center Australian Walkabout Exhibit Balloon Animal Stand Basket Gifts (Basket Shop) Bear House Breeding Center (Panda Research pack) Cairo Cafe Candy Cane Shop Chicken Stand Chocolate Gift Shop (Chocolate Bunny Shop; Chocolate Lovers) Christmas Store Coffee Cup Coffee Stand Country Quilt Shop Cultural House Egg Décor Shop Egyptian Aviary Feline Research Center Ferris Wheel Fancy Shelter (Alien 2 Planet Shelter) Game Of Chance / Skill (Bunny Ring Toss) Giggling Geisha Gingerbread Shop Globe Hotel Health Food Stand (Carrot Stand) Highland Hotel Hot Beverage Stand Hot Chocolate Stand (genki, toodlepops) Hotel House Of Eggs (Easter Egg Hunting) I-Max Theatre Igloo Gift Shop Lighthouse Loco Coco Lounge Madagascar Amphibian House Mauna Loa Cafe Moorish Towers (Golden Guard Tower, Ivory Guard Tower, Moorish Tower) Oasis Hotel & Resort Observation Tower Panda Research Center (Panda Research pack) Popcorn Stand Pretzel Stand Relaxing Ride (Chick Ride) Resort Cottage (Easter Resort Cottages Pack) Roaring Lion Retreat Santa’s Castle Santa’s Workshop Shelter Annex Shroom House Slide (Kids Slide) Small Bear House Snack Stand (Easter Cupcake Stand) South American Expedition Exhibit South American Ruins Souvenir Maker Spinning Ride (Faberge Egg Ride) Sustainable Living Center Swim Club Tall Reef Aquarium Tent The Mug Townhouses Tropical Sherbet Stand Tunnel Entrance (Grass Tunnels) Waystation Wallaby

    Fences: Bamboo Fence Black and White Curb Walls Cliff Wall Decorative Fence Decorative Ruins Wall (Decorative Ruin Walls Part 1, Decorative Ruin Walls Part 2 DD) Decorative Wall Gingerbread Fence Long Life Fence Low Decorative Wall Tall Decorative Wall

    Shelters: Asian Shelters Baobab Tree Shelter Barn shelter Burrow Shelter Cave Shelter Fancy Shelter (Panda Research pack, Easter Egg Shelters) Gate Shelter (Panda Research pack) Natural Shelter Rustic Shelter (Panda Research pack) Shelter For All Animals (DD Shelters For All Animals Pack, MM Shelters For All Animals Pack, ZT Shelters For All Animals Pack) Tunnel Shelter Entrance (Grass Tunnels)

    Other: 2012 Victorian Valentine Quilt Cottage (for sold items but not building) Catch of the Day - unreleased description for scenario maps used in freeform mode guest thought for when guests look at details of an object Leaping Leopard Restaurant (for sold items but not building) Mexican food and drink sold items Pernese Green Dragon

    Instructions: Extract LANG–ZTCDD.dll from LANG–ZTCDD.zip and put LANG–ZTCDD.dll in Zoo Tycoon’s main folder, which is the same folder that has ZT’s lang0.dll.
