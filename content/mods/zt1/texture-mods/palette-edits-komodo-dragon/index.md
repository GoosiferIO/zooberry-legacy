A small mod that edits the palette and various related graphics for the Komodo dragon, removing the purple colour scheme for a more natural one.

Made by request! All animal graphics have had their palette changed to be a warm orange-brown tone. The animal adoption icons, list icons and plaque have all been updated to match the current changes.

Should be compatible with other hacks or mods that change the Komodo .ai file, as these changes are purely graphical.
