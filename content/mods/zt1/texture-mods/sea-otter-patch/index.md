A mod that edits the palette and various related graphics for the Southern sea otter to resemble the real animal, with two approaches to continue using the original brown palette.

Have you ever looked at a real sea otter and noticed how they bear zero resemblance to the brown-bodied, tan-faced sea otters of ZT1 and ZT2? Here is your fix (for the first game, that is). 

This patch recolours all graphics associated with the Southern sea otter. The original colours are reused for another sea-faring otter – the marine otter, whose pelage is actually brown. The marine otter can also perform in shows.

Want to have a close-to-life sea otter without touching the vanilla Southern sea otter? Have the recolour as a Northern sea otter instead. The version has nearly identifcal preferences as the Southern sea otter.

Both versions are compatible with other animal configuration hacks affecting the Southern sea otter.
