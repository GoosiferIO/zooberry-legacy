A small mod that edits the palette and various related graphics for the grizzly bear, removing the yellow-green hue of the original for a more natural look.

All animal graphics have had their palette changed to a grayish brown to resemble the “grizzly” namesake without affecting the ripple colouration. The animal adoption icons, list icons and plaque have all been updated to match the current changes.

Compatible with other animal configuration hacks affecting the grizzly bear, as the changes are all graphical. While the hack can be used in an installation without Marine Mania, the icon will still remain as the hybrid coniferous/aquatic icon which was introduced in said expansion.
