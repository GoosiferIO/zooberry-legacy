A small mod that edits the palette and various related graphics for the Pacific giant octopus, changing its default yellow palette to a red palette resembling the real animal.

All animal graphics have had their palette changed to red. The ink during its ink ejection animation has been changed from the base indigo blue to a much darker and less saturated tone. The animal adoption icons, list icons and plaque have all been updated to match the current changes. 

While I have made multiple versions changing the camouflage skins, the release version keeps changes at a minimum: the pale yellow camouflage is shifted to a pale pinkish red to match the new default palette, while the other two camouflage skins are unchanged.

Compatible with other animal configuration hacks affecting the Pacific giant octopus, as the changes are all graphical.
