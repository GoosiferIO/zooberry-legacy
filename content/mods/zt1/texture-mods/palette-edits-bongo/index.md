A small mod that edits the palette and various related graphics for the Bongo, making it less saturated and more like the real animal. 

Female and young graphics have had their saturation decreased, while males have been given their own palette which is darker in colour. The animal adoption icons, list icons and plaque have all been updated to match the current changes. 

Should be compatible with other hacks or mods that change the Bongo .ai file, as these changes are purely graphical.
