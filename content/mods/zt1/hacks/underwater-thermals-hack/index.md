Did you know that the ocean floor can still spew lava even under all that water? This hack makes a number of thermal scenery items from Dinosaur Digs tank-placeable, reduces their footprint for easier landscaping, and makes them exhibit neutral. The geysers and steam vents function as makeshift hydrothermal vents in the abscene of proper vents in Zoo Tycoon.

Some of them are also indestructible now if you want to place them in your rampage-prone dinosaur exhibit.

Affected Items:
- Small Geyser
- Large Geyser
- Steam Vent
- Lava Pit
- Small Volcano
- Large Volcano
