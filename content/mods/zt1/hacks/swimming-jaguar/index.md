A patch for the base game jaguar that allows it to swim in both water terrain and tanks. Jaguars are quite renowned for their ability to swim and dive and they actively hunt by the waterside, so I made this patch in order to make the animal a little more dynamic. Both adults and young jaguars can now swim in water terrain as well as tanks, though their animations are a little limited as I can't make them from scratch. They are edited somewhat though to be a bit more dynamic, hopefully they're not so jarring.

This mod edits the jaguar.ai and as such will conflict with any other mod that alters the same file in any way. You can in theory use this mod without Marine Mania, though I haven't really tested it.
