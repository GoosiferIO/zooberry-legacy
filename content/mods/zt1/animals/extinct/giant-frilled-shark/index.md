The extinct Proteothrinax goliath is a fascinating member of the enigmatic family of frilled sharks, an ancient lineage of cartilaginous fish that has intrigued paleontologists for decades. While modern frilled sharks (Chlamydoselachus) are relatively small and elusive deep-sea dwellers, P. goliath was a prehistoric behemoth that dwarfed its modern relatives, possibly exceeding even great white sharks in length.
