Edestus, often called the scissor-toothed shark, was a genus of prehistoric cartilaginous fish that swam the oceans during the Late Carboniferous period, approximately 300 million years ago. Known for its bizarre and unique dental structure, Edestus stands out as one of the most peculiar predators in Earth's ancient marine ecosystems. Its fossils provide a fascinating glimpse into the evolutionary experimentation of early sharks and their relatives.
