Dunkleosteus was one of the most fearsome predators to ever roam the oceans. Living during the Late Devonian period, approximately 382 to 358 million years ago, this prehistoric fish belonged to the placoderm class, characterized by its armored body and formidable bite. Dunkleosteus has become one of the most iconic marine predators of its time, earning the nickname 'the T-Rex of the seas'.
