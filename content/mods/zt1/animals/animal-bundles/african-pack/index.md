- Aardwolf (Made by me, you can adopt this species of hyena for your zoo now!)
- African Clawless Otter (Made by me, finally was able to make an animal out of the otter)
- African Forest Elephant (Made by me, the subspecies of the African Elephant that isn't as known as its bush cousin is now available for your zoos!)
- African Penguin (Made by me, another great animal has decided to be a adopted animal for your zoo!)
- African Wild Ass (Made by me, this animal has custom donkey sounds I'm sure you'll love it)
- Brown Hyena (Made by me, yet another hyena species has come to be adopted by your zoos.)

- Quagga (Made by me, the recently extinct Quagga is now up for adoption at your zoos)
- Sand Cat (Made by me, yet another great little cute cat has entered the zoo tycoon dimension)
- Vervet Monkey (Made by me, I just decided that a smaller species of monkey deserved to be in ZT1)
- Walia Ibex (Made by me, this animal is compatible with the Gelada that is in the Animals of the World pack!)
- West African Dwarf Crocodile (Made by me, one of the smallest crocodiles is now available to be adopted)
