Thanks for downloading CryptoCrisis - Congo Uncovered!

# Content List

- KasaiRex (King of Kasai River)
- EmelaNtouka (Elephant slayer)
- KongaMato (Boat crusher)
- MbieluMbieluMbielu (Very very very plated)
- MokeleMbembe (River halter)
- NgumaMonene (Huge python)

All of them are highly compatible with each other, with slight terrain preference differencies.

MbieluMbieluMbielu, MokeleMbembe & NgumaMonene are tankable animals.

I started CryptoCrisis as a project series that tries to gather the cryptids from several regions of the world. Expect more soon!

Also, this pack is a Pride Month present. Each animal got its eyes with a colour of the diversity flag.

Just like previous projects, in this first part you'll find a short story divided in six sections in the animal information plaque. Enjoy!

All of them like *in-game tribal mask* and most items from Genki's tribal pack available here:
https://zootekphoenix.com/forums/files/file/1306-tribal-pack-by-genkicoll/
