From towering predators and gentle herbivores to soaring pterosaurs and marine reptiles, this pack brings the incredible diversity of ancient life to your zoo.

# Contents

The Dino Danger Cretaceous collection is composed of three parts. 

## Part 1
- Utahraptor
- Parasaurolophus
- Muttaburrasaurus
- Maiasaura
- Dakotaraptor
- Corythosaurus

## Part 2
- Protoceratops
- Oviraptor
- Ouranosaurus
- Giganotosaurus
- Deinocheirus
- Baryonyx

## Part 3
- Homalocephale
- Carcharodontosaurus
- Pteranodon
- Protarchaeopteryx
- Torosaurus
- Tylosaurus
