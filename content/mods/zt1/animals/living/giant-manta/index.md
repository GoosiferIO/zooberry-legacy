The Giant Manta Ray is the largest species of ray, known for its graceful swimming and impressive wingspan, which can exceed 20 feet. Found in tropical and subtropical waters worldwide, it feeds on plankton by filter-feeding as it glides through the ocean. Despite its size, it is gentle and poses no threat to humans.
