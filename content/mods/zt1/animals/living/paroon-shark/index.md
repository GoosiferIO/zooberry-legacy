The Paroon Shark, also known as the Iridescent Shark Catfish, is a large freshwater fish found in Southeast Asia’s rivers. Despite its name, it’s not a true shark but gets its name from its sleek body and active swimming style. It’s popular in aquariums when young but can grow over 4 feet long in the wild.
