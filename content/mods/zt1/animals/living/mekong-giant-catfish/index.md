The Mekong Giant Catfish is one of the world’s largest freshwater fish, native to the Mekong River basin in Southeast Asia. Known for its massive size and peaceful nature, this endangered species is a bottom-dwelling herbivore that can grow over 10 feet long and weigh several hundred pounds.arance, it is a shy bottom-dweller that feeds on crustaceans and small fish.
