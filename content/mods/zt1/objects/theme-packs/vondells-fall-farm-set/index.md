A set of autumn items inspired by some fall and halloween traditions from the rural midwest United States.  

Create custom corn mazes for your guests with new animated corn stalk foliage tiles, decorate your halloween areas with hay bales, hay wagons, and a pumpkin row decorative fence, and even offer your guests the opportunity to purchase some fall flowers at a flower-selling stand to take home.  

Also included is a special mystery item, which you may find at the end of your scenery menu.
