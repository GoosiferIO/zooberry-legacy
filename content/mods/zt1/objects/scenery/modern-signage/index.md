A small pack with more modern signage for your zoos! This pack includes generic signage in three sizes, as well as several signs corresponding to base game habitats, with some bonus biomes thrown in for fun. While these were made to replace the singular, somewhat outdated base game sign, these signs cannot be interacted with by guests, but they do make a nice aesthetic alternative.
