A mod that adds in more modern looking log fences for use in your zoos. These come in a few varieties, with full, windowed and low exhibit fences as well as a low decorative version for landscaping and a zoo wall variant for any aspiring map makers.

These are more durable than the usual wood slat fences but they can still be climbed out of and broken quite easily by stronger animals and various dinosaurs. As they reuse vanilla strings they will unlock at the same time the vanilla wood slat fences do too.
