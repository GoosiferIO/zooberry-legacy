This Pack contains parts to make a modern enclosure for Giraffes and Thomson gazelles. The design was taken from the Safari theme. So the parts are a expansion of the main game.

The heart of the pack is the visitor passable Giraffe House. The animals can be viewed at an eye level. Next door are stables for Thomson gazelles. Between the two animal houses is a common area. Special gates allow the giraffes and gazelles to seperate from each other in their stables.

Snowleo's Zoo Equipment hopes you have a good time building your virtual zoo's with SZE-Objects.
